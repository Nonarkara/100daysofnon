## STYLE LOCKS — the two visual languages

The two worlds must look like they come from different civilizations. Never blend them.

### 2026 — THE SIMULATION (warm, human, photographic)
- **Surface:** warm cream paper (`#f6f1e8`), grain, photographic
- **Mood:** humid, intimate, first-person, alive
- **Type:** Literata serif, generous leading
- **Accent:** Bangkok rain-amber (`#c2741a`)
- **Illustration treatment:** mono-noir — black-and-white photographic portrait-poster, deep charcoal, high contrast, subtle grain. The figure is present, embodied, looking. NO clinical, NO diagrammatic.
- **Palette words:** charcoal, smoke, skin, wet concrete, amber light

### 3026 — THE ARCHIVE (cold, clinical, archival, machine)
- **Surface:** cold graphite (`#0d0f12`), scanlines, mono
- **Mood:** sterile, measured, third-person, observing
- **Type:** JetBrains Mono, tight, uppercase labels, redaction bars
- **Accent:** Hub ice-cyan (`#7dd3fc`) — NEVER neon green/lime
- **Illustration treatment:** soft-analog-future editorial — cream paper (`#fdfcf9`), strict grid, thin rules, translucent pale-blue data panels, crosshair marks, single botanical accent. NOT a dark dashboard. NOT neon. An archive printed on warm cream.
- **Palette words:** cream, ice, measurement, absence, one dried flower

## PLACEMENT PHILOSOPHY — illustrations as punctuation, not banners

An illustration is a **chapter's pivotal moment made visible**, placed where it happens — not a decorative header.

- **Anchor the plate to the paragraph where the event occurs.** The flower plate sits beside the lines where she holds it. The four-seconds plate sits beside the 12.3 cm crossing. The reader's eye arrives at the image exactly when the prose arrives at the moment.
- **Mirror across the gutter.** In any chapter, the 2026 plate and the 3026 plate face each other across the central axis. The reader's eye does the "moving between worlds" work physically — the warm portrait on the left, the cold archive panel on the right, the same instant from two sides.
- **One plate per layer per chapter** (two plates total, facing each other). Restraint is the point. The image is a held breath, not a stream.

| Chapter | 2026 plate (warm, left) | 3026 plate (cold, right) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The man at the window, 2:14am — "none of this is real" | The row of chairs, the last one occupied, the flower |
| 2 | The man at the laptop, screen-glow, 3:14am Vienna call | The distorted clock, time compression 1:8,760 |
| 3 | The shape of absence — a gap in a group photo | Cognitive decline curves on cream paper |
| 4 | Phone face-down, June 1st, morning light | Empty cradles diminishing on archive shelving |
| 5 | The woman on the bicycle, 2:47am, looking up | Brain sector activity, two sectors lit cyan |
| 6 | Cremation smoke rising into white sky | Resonance scatter-plot, one outlier circled |
