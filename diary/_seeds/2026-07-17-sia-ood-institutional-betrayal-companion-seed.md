## The Sia Ood arc — structural only

**Rise.** Born 1971, poor family in Rayong, dropped out after M.3 (grade 9). Entered the amulet trade in Hat Yai, rose to become #1 in the Thai amulet-production industry ("พุทธพาณิชย์" — Buddhist commercialism) by his early 30s. Chief lay collaborator of a famous senior abbot (referred to in the article as *Luang Por A*). Generated hundreds of millions of baht in revenue for temples via industrial-scale amulet production, sponsored the construction of monasteries, schools, hospitals, a Buddhist university, funded 57 scholarship students including Chulalongkorn medical students and Chulachomklao military-academy cadets, personally donated over ฿3 billion across his career. Lived lavishly. Was sponsor and patron to entertainment-industry figures.

**Fall.** Late 2007 — created the *"Phra Somdet Nuea Hua"* amulet using the royal crown emblem, claiming royal-flower material and channeling proceeds through Luang Por A's foundation. Within days, Royal Household Bureau filed a complaint. Court convicted Sia Ood alone (5 years imprisonment) for fraud + unauthorized use of royal insignia. Luang Por A denied all involvement — despite (per Sia Ood's suicide letters) having initiated the project, signed the appointment, approved the venue, chaired the consecration ceremony, and opened 4 bank accounts for the proceeds. Sia Ood chose not to submit the evidence that would have implicated Luang Por A, in order to protect him.

**Aftermath in prison.** Attempted suicide, unsuccessful. Released June 2015 after ~5 years. Never visited or contacted by Luang Por A during the entire imprisonment (who during that period was promoted to Somdet Phra Rachakhana — the very promotion Sia Ood's silence was intended to protect).

**Post-release.** Traveled to visit the many charitable buildings he had funded. **Multiple institutions had removed his name plaques.** The Buddhist university (recipient of ฿186M over 8 years) refused to receive him. His hometown temple's abbot refused to testify at his trial. The abbot at a Samut Prakan temple whose ordination hall he had built refused to see him. The famous singer he had supported (cash, house-redemption) never visited in prison and denied knowing him. **Not one of the 57 scholarship recipients — including the medical students who had written him letters saying they wanted to meet him — put on their graduation gowns to take a photo with him after graduating.** The only institution that never abandoned him was **Lopburi Cancer Hospital**, which had built an MRI facility with his donation, remained in contact throughout his imprisonment, and named a meeting room after him.

**Public loss of faith.** In a widely-viewed Woody Kerdmakuy TV interview after release: *"Hell, heaven, merit, sin — I don't believe they exist. It's all fabricated. What is sin? I did massive merit-making, and this is my result."* Also: *"I don't resent the court. I resent the person I loved and respected. I did so much for him. In the end I was Hanuman carrying Lanka alone."*

**Death.** 30 October 2015. Phitsanulok hotel room. Age 44. Four months after release. ฿200 in his pocket. Sleeping-pill overdose. Multiple suicide letters left behind, one containing the detailed account of Luang Por A's involvement summarized above.

**Extortion coda.** During prosecution, Thai justice-system officials attempted to extort him — one demanded ฿13M as a "processing fee" to unfreeze assets. Not the primary story but relevant context on the institutional environment.
