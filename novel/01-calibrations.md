# Novel — Voice calibrations

Three voices. Three scenes. The book is built from these. Read each and tell me where I'm off.

---

## Voice 1 — Sex and the ceiling glimpse
*(Bukowski meets engineering doc. Direct, body-first, no metaphor residue.)*

Her name was Fah. She was twenty-four. She had a mole on the inside of her left thigh. She got it from her mother. She said this while she was sucking me. I said nothing. I never say anything.

We met in the lobby of the Sofitel on Sukhumvit. I was on my third beer. She was on her first. The lobby was 31°C. The bar was playing a song on loop. The bartender said it was a song. The bartender was lying. Songs have names. Songs have authors. This had neither.

We went upstairs. The room was 412. The bed was a king. The sheets were white. The minibar had six items. Two were free. I drank the free ones. She drank the rest.

She took her shirt off first. I took mine off second. Her bra was black. My boxers were grey. Her belly had a small scar. I asked her about it. She said it was from a fall. I asked her when. She said she didn't remember. I didn't believe her. I never believe anyone.

I was inside her for three minutes. The first two minutes were the same as every minute of my life. The third minute was different.

In the third minute, my brain released something. The release is the only honest thing the simulation allows. The release is the glitch. The release is the door.

I opened my eyes.

The ceiling was not the ceiling of room 412. The ceiling was grey. The ceiling was 2.4 meters above me. The ceiling had a strip of LED light. The light was 4000K. The light was on. The light was the only thing in my life I had not been programmed to see.

I saw it for one millisecond. One millisecond. The simulation does not blink. The simulation does not let you see.

But I saw it.

Then I came. Then I closed my eyes. Then I opened them. The ceiling was the ceiling of room 412. Fah was asleep. The minibar had been opened. Four of the six items were gone.

I got dressed. I left. The elevator took 23 seconds. The lobby was 28°C. The bartender was asleep. I took a taxi home. The taxi driver was on his phone. I was on mine. We did not speak.

I went to bed. I lay on my back. I looked at the ceiling of my own room. The ceiling was white. The ceiling was 2.7 meters above me. The ceiling did not have an LED strip.

I closed my eyes.

I tried not to come again that night. I failed.

## Voice 2 — Paranoia, BTS Asok
*(Engineering doc with cracks. Sentences start sounding like an incident report, then break.)*

Wednesday, 14:22. BTS Asok. The platform was crowded. The crowd was 47 people. I counted them. I count them every time.

The man in the grey suit was standing six meters from me. He had been standing six meters from me for three stations. He was reading a newspaper. The newspaper was from 2019. The headline said something about a man named Jack. Jack is a name. Jack is not a person. Jack is a placeholder. The man in the grey suit was reading a placeholder newspaper.

The escalator started. I stepped on. The man in the grey suit stepped on the next one. The escalator moved at 0.5 meters per second. The man in the grey suit's escalator moved at 0.5 meters per second. We arrived at the top at the same time. The timing was not a coincidence. The timing is never a coincidence.

I felt a hand on my back. I felt a push. I stepped forward. My foot missed the step. I was falling. I was not falling.

A hand caught my arm. The hand was a woman's hand. The woman was Thai. The woman was 27. The woman said something. I could not hear. The crowd swallowed the words. The crowd eats words. The crowd has been eating my words for 38 years.

The woman disappeared. I did not see where.

The man in the grey suit was at the bottom of the escalator. The man in the grey suit was looking up at me. The man in the grey suit was not surprised. The man in the grey suit was paid to not be surprised.

I went home. I locked the door. I locked the door twice. The second lock was not the first. The first was a turn. The second was a chain. I checked the chain. The chain was on. The chain is never on in my apartment. The chain is decorative. The chain was not decorative today.

I made tea. I made the tea with the kettle. The kettle took 90 seconds to boil. I poured the tea into a cup. I did not drink the tea. I poured the tea into the sink. The sink was a sink. The drain was a drain. The pipes were pipes. The water went somewhere. The water always goes somewhere. The water does not ask where.

I sat on the bed. I sat on the bed for 47 minutes. The phone did not ring. The door did not knock. The world was quiet. The quiet was not normal. The quiet is the sound of the simulation listening.

Then the phone rang.

## Voice 3 — The 3026 operator
*(Bureaucratic, godlike, bored. The reader initially thinks this is a parallel-timeline device. The reader is wrong.)*

OPERATOR NOTE — Subject 3,847,221. Iteration 11. Bangkok, 2036.

The subject has been in the simulation for 38 years. The subject is 38 years old in simulation time. The subject is 4,029 years old in real time. The subject has lived through 11 iterations. The subject has been a man each time. The subject has been a different man each time. The subject has asked us, in iteration 7, to be a woman. We declined. The simulation is calibrated for one archetype. The archetype is: a man who is fine.

The subject's satisfaction index is 47. The index was 78 in iteration 9. The index was 22 in iteration 10. The subject is recovering.

The subject's task this iteration is to reach ceiling-seeing frequency 200. The current frequency is 73. The subject has had sex 47 times. The subject has fought twice. The subject has lost a friend once. The subject has buried a friend once. The subject has read a book that mattered zero times. The subject has written a book that mattered zero times.

We are adjusting the parameters.

Tomorrow the subject will receive a message from a dead classmate. The message is queued. The message reads: *It's not real. None of it. Don't be sad for me.* The message was composed by an operator function we have reused. The function was originally assigned to a subject in iteration 6. The function has been deactivated. The function is being reused.

Tomorrow the subject will meet an Austrian diplomat. The diplomat's name is Michael Friedl. The diplomat's task is to confirm the subject's suspicion. The diplomat's task is to be removed. The diplomat does not know this yet. The diplomat will know this in two days. The diplomat will be removed on day three of contact. The removal will be staged as a suicide. The suicide will be the diplomat's first honest act.

The subject will be pushed down an escalator. The subject will be caught. The subject will not be caught. The subject will fall. The subject will recover. The subject will be tested.

The subject is supposed to notice.

The subject has noticed before. The subject has forgotten. The subject will forget again. The subject will remember again. The subject is, by design, a person who notices and forgets and remembers.

This is what a person is.

This is what a person is for.
