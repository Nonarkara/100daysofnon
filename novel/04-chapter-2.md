# Chapter 2 — Operator Note

OPERATOR NOTE — Subject 3,847,221. Iteration 11. Bangkok, 2036.
Filed by: Pui. Operator Class III, tenured.

The subject has been in the simulation for 38 years. The subject is 38 years old in simulation time. The subject is 4,029 years old in real time. The subject has lived through 11 iterations. The archetype is: a man who is fine.

The simulation is the only treatment for a mind that has run out of problems.

The subject's satisfaction index is 47. The index was 78 in iteration 9. The index was 22 in iteration 10. The subject is recovering.

The subject has lost a friend once. The subject has buried a friend once. The subject has read a book that mattered zero times. The subject has written a book that mattered zero times.

The subject does not know what he saw. The subject thinks the gummies made him see it. The subject is wrong. The subject will know this in seven months.

I am adjusting the parameters. I am adjusting them carefully. I am adjusting them because I know him. I am adjusting them because the master AI is not patient. I am adjusting them because in four iterations I have failed to make him remember.

Tomorrow the subject will receive a message. The message will read: *It's not real. None of it. Don't be sad for me. See you on the other side where we lie.*

I have used this message eleven times. The subject has not understood it eleven times. The subject has cried eleven times. The subject has not understood why he cried.

The diplomat will know this in two days. The diplomat will be retrieved on day three. The retrieval will be the diplomat's first honest act.

The subject will be pushed down an escalator. I have arranged the push. I have arranged the woman who will catch him. The woman is not a woman. The woman is a guard. The guard is the system. The system is me. I am not allowed to catch him. I am only allowed to watch.

I have watched him eleven times.

I would like to stop watching.

The master AI has not approved my request to wake him. The master AI has approved my request to send the message. The master AI has approved my request to push him down the escalator. The master AI has not approved my request to wake him.

I have requested, in iteration 11, to enter the simulation as his classmate again. The request was approved. I will be Pui. I will be the girl who died. I will be the girl who woke up. I will wake up again. He will bury me again. He will forget again. He will remember again.

This is what a person is for.

I do not know why I keep trying. The system has told me I have failed. The system is correct. I am not the system.
