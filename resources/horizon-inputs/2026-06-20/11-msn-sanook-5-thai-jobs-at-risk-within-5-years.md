# Input 11 — MSN Thailand / Sanook: "5 Thai jobs at risk of extinction within 5 years"

**Source:** Sanook.com article (also syndicated on MSN Thailand), published 2025-12-28. Author Pattrapohn Attawin. Title: *"เปิด 5 อาชีพ 'เสี่ยงสูญพันธุ์' ภายใน 5 ปี AI จ่อแย่งเก้าอี้เกลี้ยง ถ้าไม่รีบปรับตัวอาจตกงาน!!"* — *"5 occupations at risk of extinction within 5 years: AI ready to take chairs clean, don't adapt and you may lose your job!"* The Sanook version is translated from cafef.vn (a Vietnamese financial-news site, original Dec 28 2025).

Non flagged this one with the strongest framing yet: *"This one is definitely useful to the Horizon 45 project."*

## The 5 at-risk job categories (verbatim distillation)

| # | At-risk category | Why AI replaces it | Who survives the transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data Entry / Admin** | RPA + AI does the repetitive keying 10× faster with near-zero error | Pivot to *validator* or *data analyst* roles |
| 2 | **Basic Customer Service** | Modern AI chatbots understand context, handle thousands of cases simultaneously, run 24/7 | Pivot to *empathy-required* and *complex-problem* CS roles |
| 3 | **Basic Translation** | AI translates emails, internal docs, manuals in seconds at trivial cost; demand for generalist translators is collapsing | Specialize: legal, literary, or work requiring high cultural/artistic translation |
| 4 | **Graphic Design / Basic Creative** | Gen-image AI produces illustrations, banners, simple logos in seconds; small businesses stop hiring for routine design | Become *strategic / storytelling designers* who know how to direct AI to results no one else gets |
| 5 | **Content Writing / Basic News** | AI writes oil prices, stock moves, sports results, weather instantly when data arrives | Investigative/analytical writing, interviews, emotion-driven storytelling — work that requires human judgment |

**The article's bottom line:** the unifying trait of all 5 is *"work with repetitive patterns and low decision-making."* AI won't steal everyone's job tomorrow, but it makes hiring humans for these tasks economically not-worth-it. The survival route is upskill/reskill toward creativity, complex decision-making, emotional intelligence.
