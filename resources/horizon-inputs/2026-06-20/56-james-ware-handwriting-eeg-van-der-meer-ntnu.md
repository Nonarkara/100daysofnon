## Verification — the underlying claim is real, the framing is coach-marketing

**The science is real and well-cited.** Audrey van der Meer is a real developmental neuroscientist at the **Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU)**. Her handwriting-vs-typing EEG work is genuinely published — most notably *"The Importance of Cursive Handwriting Over Typewriting for Learning in the Classroom: A High-Density EEG Study of 12-Year-Old Children and Young Adults"* (Frontiers in Psychology, 2020). The 256-electrode high-density EEG methodology is consistent with her actual lab. The "20 years" claim is plausible — she has been working in this area since the early 2000s. ✅

**The post's framing is engagement-shaped** — coach-CTA at the bottom, single-sentence newline rhythm typical of LinkedIn-engagement format.
