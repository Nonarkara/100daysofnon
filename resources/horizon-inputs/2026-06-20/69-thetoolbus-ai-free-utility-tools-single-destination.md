## What it is — verified

**Author:** **Ezekiel Adewumi** (@aaezekiel_co) — solo developer, personal site + Instagram + LinkedIn all publicly linked. Real person, real signature.

**Positioning tagline:** *"All the essential tools you'd normally pay for, now free and powered by AI — all in one place. Compress PDFs, resize images, convert files, and more. No signup, no watermark."*

**Business model:** fully free (no signup, no credit card, no watermark) + monetized by sponsored placements (Descript, Tidio, Apollo, Bill, Webflow, ElevenLabs, KrispCall, Flippa, IDrive, AdCreative, LearnWorlds, Increff) and affiliate links. Partnership agency (Carril Agency).

**Privacy claim:** most tools run entirely in-browser (files never leave device). Server-processed ones (OCR, PDF rendering) delete uploads within 3 hours. No account or email required.

- 🟡 **Sponsored-link revenue model** — heavily featured across the homepage. Site's revenue depends on affiliate conversions; may subtly bias which paid alternatives get comparative recommendation attention (though the free-tools catalog itself is unaffected).
- 🟡 **Privacy claim needs partial verification for sensitive workflows** — the *"browser-only for most, server-deleted-in-3-hours for the rest"* claim is credible for the tool categories but specific server-processed tools should be identified before uploading anything genuinely confidential (e.g., a contract PDF with client-identifying information). Recommendation: for Non's use, treat browser-only tools (compressor, resizer, converter, format-conversion) as safe; treat server-processed tools (OCR, PDF-rendering, complex conversions) as *"use non-sensitive files or test with a copy first."*

## What NOT to do

- ⚠️ **Do not upload confidential client documents to server-processed tools** without verifying which specific tools require server processing. The privacy claim is credible but the categorization is not visible per-tool.
- ⚠️ **Do not endorse the sponsored placements uncritically** — the sponsored products (Descript, Tidio, Apollo, etc.) are real products but their appearance on thetoolbus.ai is paid placement, not editorial recommendation. Horizon should not conflate the free-tools catalog with the sponsored recommendations.
- ⚠️ **Do not build any production workflow on thetoolbus.ai as a load-bearing dependency** — solo-maintainer, no SLA, sponsor-revenue-dependent. Fine for personal daily-use; not fine for a Horizon or FloodDash critical-path.
