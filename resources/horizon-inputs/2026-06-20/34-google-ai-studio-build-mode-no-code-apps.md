# Input 34 — Google AI Studio "Build" mode: natural-language to working app, free with a Google account

**Source:** Thai-language post explaining Google AI Studio's "Build" mode. Hashtags: `#AIDailyTH #GoogleAIStudio #Gemini #สร้างแอป #AIสายฟรี #NoCode #เครื่องมือAI #vibecoding`. URL: **aistudio.google.com**.

## The 5-step setup

**Step 0** — Open aistudio.google.com, log in with any Google account. No new signup, no payment, works on desktop AND mobile.

**Step 1** — Click "Build" in the left sidebar. Layout: prompt box on left, live app preview on right.

**Step 2** — Type what you want, in Thai or English. Example from the post: *"Make a personal income/expense tracker app, with an add-record button and monthly total summary."* (or *"ทำแอปจดรายรับรายจ่าย มีปุ่มเพิ่มรายการและสรุปยอดรวมแต่ละเดือน"*). For inspiration, click **"I'm Feeling Lucky"** for a random project idea.

**Step 3** — Wait briefly. The app appears in the right preview. Click and interact. Want changes? Type more: *"add a pie chart"* or *"change to green color theme."*

**Step 4** — Click **"Deploy"** to ship. Get a public URL or download the source code. **First 2 apps deploy free on Google Cloud, no credit card required.**
