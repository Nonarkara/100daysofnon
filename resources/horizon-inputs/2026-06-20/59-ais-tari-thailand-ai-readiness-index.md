# Input 59 — AIS × สอวช. × IRIS Consulting: "TARI" (Thailand AI Readiness Index) — national AI-readiness assessment ships

**Source:** MarketPlusOnline article. AIS press release quoting **กานติมา เลอเลิศยุติธรรม** (Kantima Lerlertyutham), Deputy CEO, Enterprise Business, AIS. Hashtags: `#AIS #AISAcademy #TARI #ThailandAIReadinessIndex #AIReadiness #MarketPlusOnline`.

## The substance — verbatim, translated + condensed

**Partners:**
- **สอวช.** (NSTDA — the National Science, Technology and Innovation Policy Office, under the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation / อว.)
- **AIS Academy** (AIS's enterprise-training arm)
- **IRIS Consulting** (assessment methodology partner)

**The product:** "**Thailand AI Readiness Index (TARI)**" — an AI-readiness diagnostic for Thai organizations. Positioned as a national standard.

**Kantima's headline quote:**
> *"การแข่งขันวันนี้ ไม่ได้วัดว่าใครมี AI มากกว่า แต่ใครเข้าใจ พร้อม และใช้ AI สร้างคุณค่าได้จริงมากกว่า"*
> *"Today's competition isn't measured by who has more AI, but by who understands it, is ready for it, and actually uses AI to create value."*

**The 8 dimensions TARI measures:**
1. **Strategy** (กลยุทธ์)
2. **Leadership** (ผู้นำ)
3. **Data** (ข้อมูล)
4. **Technology** (เทคโนโลยี)
5. **Personnel** (บุคลากร)
6. **Governance** (ธรรมาภิบาล)
7. **Culture** (วัฒนธรรม)
8. **Business results** (ผลลัพธ์ทางธุรกิจ)

**The 3-step process:**
- **Measure** — assess readiness
- **Benchmark** — compare against industry
- **Activate** — convert results to an execution plan

**Scale ambition:** ~40 leading organizations onboarded at launch → **5,000 organizations in year one** → **20,000 organizations** long-term. That is *national-standard-forming* scale.

## Why this is the single most policy-critical input filed in the Horizon brief so far

Horizon 45 is EAD's AI Assessment Platform. **TARI is the Thai government's parallel AI-assessment initiative, launched through a public–private partnership between AIS, สอวช. (a ministerial-level office), and IRIS Consulting.** These two initiatives now occupy the same conceptual space in the Thai AI-readiness market.

**The strategic questions Horizon must answer (fast):**

1. **Compete, complement, or partner?** — Horizon can:
   - **Compete** on curriculum depth + Honesty Layer + application-layer training (weak — TARI has government-backed distribution and 20K-org ambition)
   - **Complement** by being the *learning path* TARI's "Activate" step points to (strong — TARI diagnoses, Horizon teaches)
   - **Partner** — become an accredited "Activate" pathway on the TARI standard (strongest — but requires depa/SEIC/สอวช. engagement)

2. **The 8 TARI dimensions are the de-facto national vocabulary.** Any Horizon curriculum surface that uses different dimension names for the same concepts will look off-standard to Thai enterprise learners already in a TARI conversation. **Horizon's M1/M3/M6 dimension names should be mapped explicitly to TARI's 8.** This is not surrender; it's fluency in the emerging national standard.

3. **The "Activate" gap is Horizon's opening.** TARI ships the diagnostic and the benchmark. TARI does *not* ship the training curriculum that converts assessment results into skilled personnel. That is *exactly* Horizon's product surface. If Horizon publishes a *"From TARI Assessment to AI-Fluent Team: The Horizon Activate Pathway"* offering within 90 days, it lands directly in the funnel TARI is building.
