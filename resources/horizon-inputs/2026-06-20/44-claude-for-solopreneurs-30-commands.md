# Input 44 — "Claude for Solopreneurs: 30 commands that run your one-person business"

**Source:** Infographic listing **30 slash-commands** organized into 6 categories (5 each): MONEY · MARKETING · OPS & ADMIN · CLIENT & SALES · CONTENT · RESEARCH. Anthropic-Skill-format compatible.

## The 30 commands by category

**MONEY** — `/cash-snapshot` (reads QuickBooks/spreadsheet, 30/60/90-day outlook), `/invoice-draft` (pay-enabled invoice from project notes), `/price-check` (margin math, shows 10% rate-shift impact), `/tax-estimate` (quarterly estimate, flags missed deductions), `/expense-sweep` (scans statements, flags off-budget).

**MARKETING** — `/content-plan` (30-day calendar, LinkedIn + X), `/post-draft` (platform-ready post in your voice), `/email-sequence` (5-email nurture sequence, one purpose each, clear CTA), `/landing-page` (Problem-Agitation-Solution copy), `/ad-copy` (5 variations across short and long).

**OPS & ADMIN** — `/sop-draft` (numbered repeatable process with time estimates + mistakes to avoid), `/inbox-triage` (drafts replies to top 5 emails, tone-matched per sender), `/week-plan` (weekly list from goals + deadlines, prioritized by revenue impact not urgency), `/meeting-brief` (1-page pre-meeting brief: context, agenda, decisions needed), `/client-tracker` (pipeline of every active client: status, last contact, next action, revenue).

**CLIENT & SALES** — `/lead-brief` (prospect research before pitch, returns company signals + one tailored talking point), `/proposal-draft` (mirrors client's own words to reflect their problem), `/follow-up` (tone-matched, adjusts firmness per contact number), `/contract-check` (NDA/service-agreement review in plain English, flags non-standard clauses), `/scope-lock` (signed-off agreement defining what's IN and NOT included, prevents creep).

**CONTENT** — `/newsletter-draft` (weekly notes → ready newsletter, tight intro + one main insight + strong close), `/repurpose` (long post → 5 shorter pieces across platforms), `/caption-batch` (10 social captions for one campaign theme, schedule-ready), `/video-script` (60-second script, hook-story-insight-CTA), `/thread-writer` (one insight into a 7-post X thread).

**RESEARCH** — `/competitor-scan` (3 rivals in your niche: pricing gaps, content angles, positioning weaknesses), `/trend-brief` (what's trending in your niche this week, angles you can post about today), `/idea-validate` (stress-test new offer, returns buyer persona + objections + risks + go/no-go), `/audience-map` (ideal buyer in one page: pain points, objections, language, where they hang out), `/pricing-audit` (compare your rates against 5 similar offers, flags undercharging or overcomplicating).
