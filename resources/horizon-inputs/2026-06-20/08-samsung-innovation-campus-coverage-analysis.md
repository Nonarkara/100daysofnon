## Net coverage gaps Horizon should address

After comparing all 8 inputs across the M1–M5 + executive-judgment plan:

1. **No beginner Python on-ramp.** Samsung covers this for free. Horizon should explicitly link out rather than try to compete.
2. **No "AI in the Workplace" 101 lesson.** Samsung covers this for free. Horizon should explicitly position M1 as the post-Samsung next step — *if you've taken Samsung's AI in the Workplace, M1 is your next lesson.*
3. **No applied-vertical track yet.** Samsung has AI×Marketing. Horizon's M4 is AI×Physical-World. If Horizon wants to compete on applied verticals, the candidate verticals from Non's existing portfolio are: smart-city (Chonburi/Chula/Nakhon Si Thammarat), media (NSP), education (Horizon itself, recursively), public-sector consulting (depa/SEIC), and brand work (TKC). Decision needed.
4. **No active-learning bootcamp format.** Samsung's pedagogy is Active Learning Bootcamps — paired with online training. Horizon should match or exceed this. The "shipped artifact" column in the M1–M5 table is already the right shape; the bootcamp is the *delivery mechanism* for that artifact.

## Positioning recommendation

The three competitors now on the Horizon competitive map:

| | AIS Academy (input 6) | Samsung Innovation Campus (input 8) | The Thai-language course-seller (backlog 2026-05-27) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Paid enterprise | Free CSR | Paid community |
| **Audience** | Corporate enterprise | Thai youth + students | Small biz / creators |
| **Format** | In-house training | Online + bootcamps | Online community + courses |
| **Depth** | Conservative, Copilot-centered | Coding fundamentals + AI literacy | Workflow recipes |
| **Agentic coverage** | None | None | Light (4-agent YouTube pipeline) |
| **Executive judgment** | None | None | None |
| **Physical-world** | None | Mentions IoT in program-level | None |

**Horizon's wedge is clear:** the *agentic-AI + executive-judgment + applied-vertical* slice of the field that none of the three competitors covers. Samsung is the FREE entry-level on-ramp for foundational Python and AI literacy. AIS is the paid enterprise standard for Copilot-era productivity. The course-seller is the paid Thai-language community for hands-on workflow recipes. **Horizon should be the place where someone goes after Samsung and before — or instead of — buying an AIS in-house engagement, when they want to build agents and learn to override AI judgment, not just comply with it.**

That positioning leaves the M1–M5 plan from the brief intact and adds three explicit positioning callouts to it:

- Prerequisite linkout: *"new to Python? Take Samsung Innovation Campus first, then come back to M1."*
- Differentiator vs AIS: *"AIS teaches Copilot productivity. Horizon teaches you to compose multiple AI as a team and to know when to override them."*
- Differentiator vs the course-seller: *"course-seller teaches you specific workflows for content. Horizon teaches you the underlying patterns so you can build any workflow."*
