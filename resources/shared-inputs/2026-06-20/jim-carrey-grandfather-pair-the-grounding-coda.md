## Why I am filing this here, not in any project folder

The 47 prior inputs today are about *how AI works, what tools exist, how to build, how to monetize, how to teach.* The Jim Carrey quote + the grandfather photo are about *who gets to be in that conversation at all.*

The grandfather in the photo is not a Horizon learner. He is not a SLIC stakeholder. He is not in any market segment the day's brief addresses. He is exactly the kind of person whose existence the brief's frameworks make invisible — a non-targeted, non-monetizable, non-upskillable life that the AI economy will not reach in any direction.

**Non's comment is a grounding observation. It deserves to be filed as such, not as content to be slotted into a curriculum frame.**

## What I will not do with this input

- I will not propose it as a Horizon recruiting-frame anchor. The grandfather is not a Horizon prospect; turning his story into marketing is exactly the move Non's comment is rejecting.
- I will not propose it as a Pinterest library card. The visual would work for engagement; the use of the man's image to drive enrollment would be a category error.
- I will not propose it as a Horizon Research paper anchor. The paper would need to be about him, not about the curriculum's relationship to him.

## What this DOES connect to honestly across today's brief

- **[[Input 11 — 5 at-risk Thai jobs]]** named the threat of AI-induced job loss. The grandfather is the labor-economics worst case the academic argument warns about: the worker for whom no policy intervention in the timeframe of his life will improve outcomes.

Bangkok is geologically young (sediment from the last 2,000 years); its informal-elderly-workforce safety net is institutionally young (no robust social security predates the 1990s). ## The Jim Carrey quote as the bridge between the AI-stack day and the grandfather photo

Jim Carrey's quote names the *limit case* of the kind of success the curriculum-input arc has been pointing toward. *Even at the top, the wealth-and-fame answer is not the final answer.* The grandfather's life is the *floor case* — *the absence of wealth-and-fame is also not a question of effort.*

The two together describe **the bracket within which the entire AI-economy conversation actually exists** — not as the canvas the curriculum paints on, but as the room the curriculum is being painted in. The room contains more than the canvas.
