## Verification pass — three green, two amber

- 🟢 BCG is real; "Intelligent Cities Index" is a real BCG publication line
- 🟢 The 5-domain (Outcomes / Strategy / Adoption / Ways of Working / Enablers) framework is BCG's actual methodology
- 🟢 The specific cities on the list broadly align with BCG's client-relationship reach and other credible smart-city indices
- 🟡 **Publication date not visible** — the *"Intelligent Cities Index"* has been published in multiple versions; whether this is the 2024, 2025, or 2026 edition matters for citation and comparison. Verify before Horizon or SLIC v4 cites specifically.
- 🟡 **Client-effect bias** — professional-services firms like BCG have deeper data on cities they have consulted for. Cities that hired BCG for smart-city projects may rank more accurately (either up or down) than cities BCG has no engagement with. **Bangkok's absence from top 20 may reflect either (a) genuine gap, or (b) BCG having thin data on Bangkok because BCG has not been the primary smart-city consultant to BMA / depa.** Either interpretation is policy-relevant; the first is a diagnosis, the second is a competitive-consulting-market signal.

Per [[feedback_teach_dont_silently_fix]] + [[SLIC Input 13]] methodology critique: surface the client-effect bias before adopting the ranking downstream.
